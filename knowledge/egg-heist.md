# Egg Heist — how good players run it

Strategy summarised in our own words from creators who play the mode a lot, plus the player's own
run. Evidence: `log/2026-10-26-egg-heist-strategy.md`.

## The rules that shape every run

- **Escape Condition** (on the left of the screen): Beginner, Hard and Nightmare need **either 1
  egg submitted or the team's Eggshell Coins target** — 20,000 / 60,000 / 150,000. **Chaos needs
  300,000 coins and an egg does not count.** The coin target on screen therefore also says which
  difficulty you are on.
- **Clock:** 20 minutes on Beginner, 15 on the others. Near the end a horde of enraged enemies
  floods the Sanctum and the game tells you to evacuate.
- **Faint and you lose your backpack and everything equipped** — except in your first 3 runs on
  each difficulty (Beginner Protection, which also shows the whole Sanctum map).
- **Fog:** on higher difficulties the Sanctum map starts covered, so you go in blind. The player's
  own Hard run showed "The fog there is very thick" with only the start room visible — this is
  where a layout overlay helps most.
- **Locked rooms:** Rusted Iron Key (Beginner), Silver-Plated (Hard), Gilded (Nightmare and Chaos).
  A vendor inside the run, usually near an exit, also sells keys. Key rooms are often guarded.
- **First successful extraction of the day gives double rank points.**

## The egg

1. At a nest, **dig** to reveal the egg. **Extra enemies often spawn right after** — and Alpha
   guardians tend to appear once a first egg is taken. Clear them before moving.
2. **Twine** with the egg and roll it. While rolling you cannot fight.
3. **Umbral egg:** take it straight to the boat. **Ordinary egg:** some players roll it to the
   nearest Sanctum exit, leave it by the gate, explore more, and collect it on the way out.
4. At the boat: **Deliver Egg**, then **Safe Evacuation** — two separate actions. Delivered eggs
   arrive by mail and are shared with the whole team.

## Habits the guides agree on

- **Plan the way back first.** Note the door you entered by and the route to the boat; mark the
  nearest exit before rolling an egg so you never stop to open the map.
- **Confirm the layout at the next junction** — if it doesn't match, switch maps at once.
- **Leave with time to spare.** Under about 2 minutes left, stop looting and move.
- **Don't get greedy.** One more key room with a full bag is how runs are lost.
- **Loot by value per kilogram;** on Chaos take only gold. Weight slows you down stage by stage
  (very light → overloaded), and the safe still counts towards your load.
- **Don't sell essences, keys, gear or unregistered collectibles** at the end-of-run sale.
