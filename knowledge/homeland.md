# Homeland, the RV and the farm — daily jobs and how to get to each

What there is to do at home every day, and how the character gets to each job. Each job is marked
**menu** (done from screens, no walking), **teleport + walk** (a menu jump, then a few steps and an
interact button) or **walk**. Evidence: `log/2026-10-25-homeland.md`.

## The three places

- **RV Park** — a shared beach park where your RV is parked next to other players'. It has the
  **Park Board** (Aniimo trips) and the chairs that give the park buff.
- **Homeland** — your own island: the farm, the facilities, your house, the order robot.
- **The RV** — the vehicle itself. Its screen is where you level it up; walking into it in the RV
  Park and through its portal ("Go to Home") takes you to Homeland.

## Getting there

**Main menu → Homeland tile** opens the **Homeland screen**: your RV in the middle, its level and
name at the top, four round buttons — **Component Upgrade** and **RV Level Up** on the left,
**Furniture Shop** and **RV Customization** on the right — and two travel buttons bottom-right:
**RV Park** and **Homeland**. One tap on either takes you there, after a loading screen.

Once in Homeland, a bar along the bottom replaces the usual action buttons:
**Aniimo · Manage · Home · Build · Warehouse**. These do most of the daily work without walking:

| Button | Opens | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Aniimo** | Aniimo Management, tabs **Aniimo** and **Food** | Which Aniimo work where (Batch Add / Batch Remove); the food store (**Add All** tops it up) |
| **Manage** | Home Management — a top-down plan of the whole island; zoom in to see every plot and facility | Tap a facility → tabs Formula · Manage · Level Up; change a recipe, **Batch overwrite**, see which ones say "Insufficient items" |
| **Home** | Home Overview — the island as zones, each with **Teleport** | Jump to a zone; Service Hub's panel has **Order Quantity** (Daily Orders), **Home Shop** and **Co-op Store** |
| **Build** | Build mode — furniture and facilities to place | Not daily |
| **Warehouse** | Your inventory beside the Home Warehouse, items dragged between them, **Batch Deposit**, Confirm | Weekly: take out Growth Fruit and Aniipod Megas |

Crops and products are collected by the working Aniimo on their own and land in the Home Warehouse
("Stored in the Homeland Warehouse!"). Nothing has to be picked by hand.

always the bottleneck), Component Upgrade (needs Modification Toolkits from your Home Index score).

## How the character gets around at home

- **Long distances never need walking.** The Homeland screen jumps to RV Park or Homeland; inside
  Homeland, **Home → a zone → Teleport** jumps to that zone.
- **The last few steps do.** The Park Board, chairs, the Bud Ticket Jar and a slacking Aniimo all
  need the character standing next to them. When close, an **interact prompt** appears beside the
  object (seen on PC as "F  Park Board", "F  Harvest Bud Tickets", "F  Go to Home"). On a phone
  it should be a tappable button in the same place — **not yet seen on a phone**.
- **A farm plot in reach** shows a small menu: Use Plant Growth Tonic · Show Details · Settings ·
  Upgrade Building. Not needed daily.

So an automated home round would be: teleport by menu, then **one short joystick drag** toward the
object, then **tap the interact prompt once it appears**. The drag only has to be roughly right
because the prompt appearing is the signal that the character arrived. That is the first walking
chore worth building, and the Bud Ticket Jar and Park Board are the two that need it.

## What to do first when automating

Jobs 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 and 10 are menu-only and can be chores today. Jobs 1 and 6 need the
teleport-plus-short-walk step. Job 2 and 13 are optional.
