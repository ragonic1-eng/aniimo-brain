## From the narration

- Escape targets 20k / 60k / 150k coins or 1 egg; Chaos 300k coins only (StatWise, Glowbugs).
- 20 min on Beginner, 15 otherwise; end-of-run enemy horde (StatWise, Glowbugs).
- Three protected runs per difficulty, map revealed during them (StatWise, Khraze).
- Fog of war on higher difficulties; "note where the key room is and how far from an exit you are"
  (Khraze).
- Plan the return to the boat from the start; confirm the layout at the next junction (StatWise).
- Dig → extra enemies; twine and roll; Deliver Egg then Safe Evacuation; eggs by mail (all three).
- Umbral eggs straight home; others parked at the nearest exit (Glowbugs).
- Leave with under ~2 minutes left (Glowbugs).
- Keys by difficulty; in-run vendor near an exit (StatWise, Khraze).
