## The converter, on this recording

`tools/video_to_chores.py` has not yet produced a usable result here. Three reasons found:
the indicator is faint and two-signed (above); the file is variable-frame-rate so frame-index
times drift by up to a second (fix: re-encode to constant 30 fps first); and any screen motion —
map pans, cards sliding — throws up dozens of "fresh round blobs". Calibration continues.
