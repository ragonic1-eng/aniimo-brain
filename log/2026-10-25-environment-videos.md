# Getting to know the game's screens, from six recent videos

**Purpose:** learn what every button on the phone HUD does and how the menus are laid out —
events, character, settings, quests, the daily screens — before any chore is written.
**Watched by:** Claude, 2026-10-25, sampled every 3 s with near-duplicates dropped.

## Why these six

The UI changes between updates. Mobile launched about **22–23 Sept 2026**, the first large update
landed **17 Oct 2026**, and another (Whisperwake Isles) is announced. So: newest mobile footage
first, launch-week only where nothing newer showed that menu. Two of the menu guides are PC or
console footage — the full-screen menus are laid out the same on every platform (checked against
the mobile settings video, below), only the HUD and the button hints differ.

## What was seen, in order of usefulness

- **Free-roam HUD on a phone** (`QwZkNkKh1JI` 0:39, `v9dAvlnpfMc` 1:00): minimap top-left with a
  quest tracker under it; three to seven icons top-right; party portraits down the right edge;
  action buttons ringed round a main button bottom-right; joystick bottom-left; camera, chat,
  emote and lock-on along the bottom-left.
- **Joystick in use** (`v9dAvlnpfMc` 0:45–1:09, `i--_CIfnszc` 1:45–1:57): while a thumb is down, a
  large faint ring with a white dot appears where the thumb landed — its centre moved between
  y 0.72 and 0.80 across frames. With no thumb down only the small four-arrow icon shows.
- **Opening the main menu on a phone** (`QwZkNkKh1JI` 0:06–0:08): the right-most top-right icon (a
  pod in a ring) turns into the menu's close X in the same spot as the menu slides in on the right.
  The finger is not visible, so the tap itself is inferred.
- **Main menu → gear → Settings** (`QwZkNkKh1JI` 0:08–0:13, `i--_CIfnszc` 0:45–1:27).
- **A "Rate Me" popup** interrupted play with Maybe Later / Confirm (`v9dAvlnpfMc` 0:48).
- **Toasts** in combat: "Insufficient EP to use this skill", "Actions too frequent"
  (`i--_CIfnszc` 2:09, 2:24).
- **Events** list and pages, **Daily Activity**, **Aniimo Challenge**, **Stamp Rush**, **Log**,
  **Elite Training**, **Map** (`sbFoIocJQa8` 0:06–10:57).
- **Harvest Moon Festival** details, **Season Achievement**, **Companion Handbook**
  (`J91CBR3gpZk` 0:57, 4:18, 6:45).
- **Profile**, **Inventory**, **Mail**, the console radial menu (`d1ZEwjXoxRY` 4:09, 6:12, 7:36,
  43:30).
