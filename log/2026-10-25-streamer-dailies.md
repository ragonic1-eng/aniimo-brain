# How regular Aniimo players do their dailies — five creators' routines

**Purpose:** learn the daily and weekly routine from people who play it every day, before drafting
chores. **Watched by:** Claude, 2026-10-25 — frames every 3 s, plus the narration where YouTube's
captions could be fetched (Kairos only; the rest were rate-limited).

## Choosing who to watch

About 50 daily-routine videos were found. Channels were ranked by how much of their recent output
is Aniimo (out of their last 30 uploads), how recent the routine video is, and whether it shows the
screens rather than only talking.

skimmed; the routine below rests on Kairos, zenitizer and Lex.

## What was seen

- **Kairos** (narrated): opens Shop → Corner Shop → **Open All** first, then Shop → **Exchange →
  Mysterious Vendor**, which he calls the first thing he does every login; its stock rotates daily
  and is the same for everyone on a server. Then spends Primegy near its 300 cap through Institute
  Guide → **Aniimo Challenge**, or the **Daily Supply Pack** (Shop → Exchange → Outpost Vendor →
  Item Exchange) as a weaker fallback. Then Homeland: Aniimo on a Trip, sitting on a chair about
  10 s for a park buff, home orders (turn in, refresh), seeds, food, bud tickets, processors, egg
  hatchers, selling excess. Then the Map: weather schedule, and the free **Nurture** (one a day,
  stacks to three). Then **Institute Guide → Daily Activity**, about 3,000 EXP a day. Then messages
  to friends, one or more **Egg Heist** runs (the first of the day gives double rank points), and
  the Events tab (Star-Crossed Tea Party is an idle catch-up for the weekly Voxel Coin and stamp
  caps). Weekly: Companion Handbook quests and its Exchange, the season shop, discounted pods at
  Outpost Vendor and Super Claw, the hardest Holo-Battle Sim, Holo-Battle Interlink, Omega
  challenges three times each, the Breezy Plains branch egg, emptying the Homeland warehouse.
- **zenitizer** (captions on screen, 0:00–20:00): the same shape, in this order — RV home
  (outgoing Aniimo, plants, orders, seeds, selling, production, friends' plants, home event,
  stars) → shop (Corner Shop, fragment exchange at Outpost Vendor, **Mysterious Vendor**, buy
  energy once) → Idyll (nurture, spend energy on Aniimo Challenge) → **Daily Activity rewards** →
  Egg Heist (6 runs, 6 Chaos tickets, gold keys as needed). The weekly list is on screen at 19:42.
- **Daily Activity, claimed** (zenitizer 17:13–17:31): yellow cards with a blue **Claim** and a red
  "!" are done but unclaimed. Each claim greys the card and moves it to the end of the row; the
  points bar rose 0 → 600; then an **Item Obtained** box, and the chests on the bar ticked. Tasks
  that day: Log In, Primegy 60, **Catch 5**, Stamps 50, Homeland.
- **Lex** (0:39): tasks that day were Primegy, Combat (defeat 5), Stamps, **Shop (buy an item from
  any shop)**, Log In. The 100-point chest carried a red gift badge once reached.
- **Shop** (Kairos 0:24–1:00): bottom tabs Monthly Pass · Pack · Appearance · Item · Corner Shop ·
  Top-Up; Corner Shop's left tabs Corner Shop and **Exchange**. Exchange is a grid of vendor cards:
  Outpost Vendor, ChillBeanz, Lumen, Mysterious Vendor, Super Claw, Coilstrike Club. The Mysterious
  Vendor page lists today's items with prices in credits; each has a **daily limit**, a quantity
  stepper with a max button, a total, and **Buy**.

— Claude, 2026-10-25
