# Claim Daily Activity

**Draft — not yet run, and not yet seen on the player's own phone.** Built from recent videos;
see `chore.json` for the app's version.

**Goal:** collect the points for daily tasks already done, then every reward chest those points
unlock. All of it together is up to 3,000 EXP and 1,200 tickets a day.

**Done when:** no yellow card with a blue **Claim** is left, and no chest on the points bar shows
a red gift badge.

**Starts and ends:** out in the world, on the normal game screen.

**This chore only claims.** It does not do the tasks themselves — log in, spend 60 Primegy,
catch or defeat 5 Aniimo, earn 50 Season Stamps, buy something in a shop, place an Aniimo at home.
Which tasks appear changes from day to day. Run this last, after the rest of the routine.

## How to read a task card

| Card looks like | Means | The chore… |
|---|---|---|
| Pale blue, "In Progress", no button | Not done yet | leaves it |
| Pale blue, dark **Go** button | Not done; Go takes you there | leaves it |
| **Yellow, blue Claim button, red !** | Done, points waiting | **taps Claim** |
| Grey, big tick, "Claimed" | Already collected | leaves it |

## Steps

1. *If* a "Rate Me" popup is up, tap **Maybe Later**.
2. Tap the **pod-in-a-ring icon** at the right end of the top-right row — the main menu opens.
3. Tap the **Institute Guide** tile (small, dark booklet icon, below Events).
4. Tap the **Daily Activity** tab — the top round tab on the blue strip at the left.
5. **Repeat:** tap a blue **Claim** on a yellow card. The card turns grey and jumps to the
   right-hand end of the row; the others slide left; the points number rises. Go round until no
   Claim is left.
6. Tap the **Daily Activity** tab once more. It changes nothing on screen — it is there so the
   app treats step 5 and steps 7–8 as two separate loops.
7. **Repeat:** tap a chest on the points bar that has a **red gift badge** → an "Item Obtained"
   box appears → 8. tap the box to close it. Go round until no chest has a badge.
9. Tap the **back arrow** top-left, then the menu's **close X** if it is still open.

## Watch out for

- **Cards move after every claim**, so the second Claim is never where the first one was. The
  step must search the whole row each time — which a repeating step does.
- **Grey Claimed and yellow Claim are the same shape.** Colour must be compared too, as the app
  already does.
- **Only 500 points are needed** for all five chests, so not every task needs doing.
- The tile and tab positions come from PC and Android footage at 16:9; on a 19.5:9 phone they
  will sit somewhat differently. They are hints only.
- Optional steps (1, and closing the menu at the end) are not yet understood by the app.
