# Claim all mail

**Draft — not yet run, and not yet seen on the player's own phone.** Built from recent videos;
see `chore.json` for the app's version.

**Goal:** collect every reward waiting in the in-game mail. Event rewards, compensation and gifts
arrive here, and each letter shows how long it has before it expires.

**Done when:** no letter shows a reward waiting, and Claim All brings no "Item Obtained" box.

**Starts and ends:** out in the world, on the normal game screen.

## Steps

1. *If* a "Rate Me" popup is up, tap **Maybe Later**.
2. Tap the **pod-in-a-ring icon** at the right-hand end of the top-right icon row. The main menu
   slides in on the right, with a close X where the icon was.
3. On the thin rail at the menu's right edge, tap the **envelope** (below the gear). The Mail
   list opens: "Mail n/200".
4. Tap **Claim All** at the bottom of the list. An "Item Obtained" box shows what came in — or
   nothing happens when there was nothing to claim.
5. *If* the box is up, tap it to close it.
6. Tap the **back arrow** top-left, then the menu's **close X**.

## Watch out for

- **"Delete Read" sits right next to "Claim All".** Find Claim All by its words, never its place.
- The envelope's position on the rail is read off one mobile video; it is a hint only.
- The app does not yet understand *optional* steps (1 and 5). Until it does, a missing popup
  would stop the chore there — so the importer must skip optional steps that are not on screen.
